About

Two systems. One engineer.

I'm an Automobile Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, specializing in Automotive Electronics. I move between two worlds that are increasingly the same vehicle: the mechanical side — chassis, powertrains, wiring — and the electronics side — microcontrollers, sensors, and the buses that let them talk to each other.

As Powertrain Head at Team 1.618 (Team Golden Ratio), I work Formula Student builds from the chassis floor to the ECU. Outside the team, I'm usually deep in a personal build — currently a holographic music/weather display running on an ESP32.
